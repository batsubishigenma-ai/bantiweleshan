✦ About Me ✦

Hello! I'm AJ, a first-year Bachelor of Science in Criminology (BSCrim) student at the University of the Cordilleras. I enjoy learning new skills and continuously improving myself, both academically and creatively.

I have experience in drawing, encoding documents, photo and video editing, and creating simple portfolio websites. I also have a growing interest in music and am currently learning how to play the guitar and piano. I believe that exploring different skills helps me become more creative, disciplined, and adaptable.

As a criminology student, I am currently studying Theories of Crime Causation, Fundamentals of Criminal Investigation and Intelligence, Human Rights Education, Readings in Philippine History, Purposive Communication, First Aid and Water Safety, IT Application Tools in Criminal Justice and Public Safety, and Technopreneurship. These subjects are helping me develop critical thinking, communication, investigative, and technical skills that will prepare me for a career in public service.

My goal is to become a firefighter in the future. I aspire to serve my community by protecting lives, responding to emergencies, and helping people in times of need. I believe that courage, discipline, compassion, and continuous learning are essential qualities for this profession. Every skill and experience I gain today brings me one step closer to achieving that dream and making a positive impact on the lives of others.
